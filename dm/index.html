Muhammad Anwarul Azim, Lecturer, Department of Computer Science and Engineering

Discrete mathematics teaches the structures and techniques that computer science is built on: logic, proofs, sets, functions, counting, relations and graphs. The course stresses mathematical reasoning and the different ways a problem can be solved.
